Pillar 1 — Technical accessibility —

robots.txt for AI bots, server-side rendering, llms.txt, sitemap, and Cloudflare AI Crawl Control.

Pillar 2 — Content citability —

CITABLE framework: BLUF, intent architecture, third-party validation, answer grounding, block structure, latest timestamps, entity schema.

Pillar 3 — Schema & entities —

JSON-LD Organization with knowsAbout / sameAs, Product / Service link-back, FAQPage for AI extraction, entity chain depth.

Pillar 4 — Off-site presence —

Wikipedia / Wikidata, G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot, Reddit organic, YouTube, LinkedIn, recent tier-1 PR.

Pillar 5 — Real-world citations —

Verified mentions in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for category intent queries.

Methodology

This audit is the output of the open-source claude-geo skill. Every pillar score is computed by a real verifier script, not a hypothetical estimate.

PillarVerifier script(s)
Technical accessibilitycheck_robots_txt.py, check_llms_txt.py, fetch_page.py --check-ssr

Schema & entitiescheck_schema.py
Off-site presencecheck_wikipedia.py, check_trust_sites.py, check_reddit_presence.py, check_youtube_presence.py
Real-world citationscheck_llm_mentions.py

Scoring rubric available in skills/geo/references/scoring-rubric.md in the plugin source.
